# Audio Assets

Put background music files here.

The story scene automatically looks for:

- `chapter-1.mp3` or `chapter-1.ogg`
- `chapter-2.mp3` or `chapter-2.ogg`
- `chapter-3.mp3` or `chapter-3.ogg`
- `chapter-4.mp3` or `chapter-4.ogg`

Recommended style for chapter 1:

- soft piano or music box
- subtle river/air ambience baked into the track
- slow romantic tempo
- gothic-romantic details for Kiara: celesta, soft bells, dark strings, faint choir pad
- loopable file, ideally 60-120 seconds
- export around -18 LUFS integrated so it stays behind dialogue

Use music you own, commissioned music, royalty-free tracks, or tracks with permission/license.
Do not use copyrighted YouTube music unless you have explicit rights to use it in a public web game.
